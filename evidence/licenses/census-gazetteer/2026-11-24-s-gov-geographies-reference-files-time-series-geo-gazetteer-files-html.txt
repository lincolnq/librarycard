 Gazetteer Files

 The U.S. Gazetteer Files provide a listing of all geographic areas for selected geographic area types. The files include geographic identifier codes, names, area measurements, and representative latitude and longitude coordinates.

 2026

 This page provides links to the 2026 gazetteer files for 120th congressional districts, American Indian/Alaska Native/Native Hawaiian Areas, census tracts, counties, county subdivisions (minor civil divisions/census county divisions), places, school districts (elementary, secondary, unified, and administrative), state legislative districts (upper and lower), states, 2020 Census urban areas, and ZIP Code tabulation areas (ZCTAs) for the 50 states, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico.

 Notes:

 All national files are in a compressed format.

 Files are pipe-delimited text with one record per file, unless noted.

 Population and housing unit counts are not included in these files.

 The Island Areas (American Samoa, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, and the U.S. Virgin Islands) are not included in these files.

 The geography in these files matches the areas for which the 2026 American Community Survey data are published.

 Because of changes in boundaries and entity names, the creation of new entities, and the dissolution of others, users should not expect a one-to-one relationship between the entity names and codes in these files and the 2013-2025 American Community Survey, 2020 Census, 2010 Census, Census 2000, or 1990 Census files.

 State Legislative Districts - Lower Chamber

 The state legislative districts - lower chamber file contains data for 49 states and Puerto Rico for the 2026 election cycle. Nebraska has a unicameral legislature, and the District of Columbia has a single council, both of which the Census Bureau treats as upper-chamber legislative areas for the purpose of data presentation. Therefore, there are no lower chamber state legislative districts provided for Nebraska or the District of Columbia.
