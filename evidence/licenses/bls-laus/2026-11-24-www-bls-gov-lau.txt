 11/20/2026

higher in 1 state, and stable in 46 states. Nonfarm payroll employment increased in

 Sept. jobless rates down over the year in 226 of 387 metro areas; payroll jobs up in 11 

 10/28/2026 

 Jobless rates were lower in September than a year earlier in 226 of the 387 metropolitan 
areas, higher in 138, and unchanged in 23. Nonfarm payroll employment increased over the 
year in 11 areas, decreased in 3, and was essentially unchanged in 373.

 Hawaii and South Dakota unemployment rates 2.2 percent in December 2025 

 In December 2025, Hawaii and South Dakota had the lowest unemployment rates, 2.2 percent each. North Dakota, Vermont, and Alabama also had unemployment rates below 3.0 percent. The District of Columbia had the highest unemployment rate, 6.7 percent. The next highest rates were in California and New Jersey, 5.5 percent and 5.4 percent, respectively.
