 Tools + Resources

 Other data sources

 This is a list of open data sources recommended by MEDSL.

 2010 Voting Districts Codes 

 National and state files containing codes for Voting Districts are available for download from the U.S. Census Bureau. Voting Districts were not provided by every state and are therefore not available for every state.

 A New Nation Votes 

 This is a searchable collection of election returns from the earliest years of American democracy.

 Constituency-Level Elections Archive (CLEA) 

 The Constituency-Level Elections Archive (CLEA) is a repository of detailed election results at the constituency level for lower house legislative elections from around the world—including the U.S.

 Daily Kos Elections' datasets 

 The ultimate guide to Daily Kos Elections datasets

 Dave Leip’s Atlas of U.S. Presidential Elections 

 This is a resource for U.S. Presidential Election results and other types of election data.

 Election Administration and Voting Survey (EAVS) 

 This is the biennial Election Administration and Voting Survey (EAVS) which collects state-by-state data on the administration of federal elections. It is conducted by the Election Assistance Commission.

 Election Geodata 

 This github provices precinct shapes (and vote results) for U.S. elections past, present, and future.

 Federal Voting Assistance Program 

 FVAP's reports and surveys provide unique insight into UOCAVA voting.

 Harvard Election Data Archive 

 This archive contains data on election results, voting behavior, and electoral politics, with particular focus on the United States. 

 Klarnerpolitics Dataverse 

 Harvard Dataverse collection by Carl Klarner with useful data and tools, including state legislative election returns from 1967 to 2012.

 Mapping Early American Elections 

 This project offers a window into the formative era of American politics by producing interactive maps and visualizations of Congressional and state legislative elections from 1787 to 1825.

 MEDSL Hosted Election Science Data 

 This Dataverse contains data that is collected by affiliates and external scholars that MEDSL is hosting and archiving.

 MEDSL New Initiatives Grants Dataverse 

 This dataverse contains the datasets produced by the New Intitiatives Grants Program sponsored by MEDSL and the Hewlett Foundation. 

 Metric Geometry and Gerrymandering Group 

 The MGGG Redistricting Lab studies applications of geometry and computing to U.S. redistricting, and builds open-source tools and resources that create public access and analytical power for better understanding districts and their consequences.

 OpenElections 

 OpenElections is working to create the first free, comprehensive, standardized, linked set of election data for the United States, including federal and statewide offices.

 PlanScore 

 PlanScore presents a comprehensive historical database of partisan gerrymandering.

 Princeton Gerrymandering Project 

 This project researches gerrymandering across the states.

 Quantifying Gerrymandering 

 A nonpartisan research group centered @ Duke Math and producing data and analysis on redistricting in the U.S.

 Redistricting Data Hub 

 A portal to access redistricting data, tools, training, and educational resources.

 Stephen Pettigrew's 2018 County Returns 

 November 2018 county-level election returns for US Senate, US House, and governor elections in each state. Also state legislative and local results in many states.

 Survey of the Performance of American Elections (SPAE) 

 This is a survey conducted in conjunction with the Elections Performance Index which gauges the quality of the election experience in the U.S.

 The Elections Archive (THEA) 

 This is a repository of subnational election results and associated materials. These data are preserved and consolidated in one comprehensive and reliable resource that is ready for analysis and publicly available at no cost. 

 The United States Election Project 

 This is a source for time-series voter turnout data at the national and state levels. The site also includes information on redistircting and voter lists.

 Voting Information Project 

 The Voting Information Project (VIP) works with states to provide official information to voters about where to vote and what’s on their ballots.
